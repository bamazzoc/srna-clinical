Mallampati Classification
▼
Class I ✓
Soft palate, uvula, fauces, pillars fully visible
Class II ✓
Soft palate, uvula, fauces visible
Class III ⚠
Only base of uvula visible — anticipate difficulty
Class IV 🔴
Soft palate NOT visible — difficult airway plan required
ASA Physical Status Classification
▼
ASA I
Healthy patient, no systemic disease
ASA II
Mild systemic disease — controlled HTN, mild asthma, BMI 30–40, social smoker
ASA III
Severe systemic disease — COPD, poorly controlled DM, morbid obesity, CAD, MI >3mo
ASA IV
Severe disease = constant threat to life — decompensated CHF, MI <3mo, ESRD
ASA V
Moribund — not expected to survive without surgery (ruptured AAA, massive PE)
ASA VI
Brain-dead organ donor
+E
Emergency modifier — delay = threat to life/limb/organ. MD sign-off required.
